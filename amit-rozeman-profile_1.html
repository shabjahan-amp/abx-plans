Summary

Amit Rozeman owns the e-commerce and product experience for the most recognizable name in American grocery — wholefoodsmarket.com, the WFM mobile app, and the digital surface that sits between $574B of Amazon-owned retail and the customer's cart. He runs that surface as VP E-Commerce And Product Management out of Austin, having moved up the WFM digital ladder since 2020: Sr Manager (Whole Foods Market On Amazon, Seattle), Sr Director PM & UX, VP PM & UX, and now VP E-Commerce. He's the Economic Buyer on the active Amplitude POC and the executive who attended Amplitude's 5/11/26 onsite at WFM Austin HQ — the meeting where, per the WFM Mutual Action Plan, budget was approved.


What makes Amit distinct is the path he took to get there. He's a two-tour Israeli Navy officer — Lieutenant turned Captain — who spent his last navy assignment as Field Doctrine And Training Lead, the role that writes the playbook before the operation runs. That's followed by Haifa University, the UVA Darden MBA (2013-2015), and a 10-year Amazon climb from In-Stock Manager in Toys & Games up through WFM. A Darden Learning Team classmate, Brandon Chinn, describes him as "naturally gifted at analytics, modeling and operations — often cracking cases far before the rest of us… a gift for teaching and communicating complex material to diverse audiences." Native Hebrew, full English. Builder, not broadcaster — minimal authored LinkedIn, sparse public surface area, almost no conference circuit. Which means the signal is in the work, the team he's assembled, and the Field Doctrine he's writing for WFM's digital next chapter.
